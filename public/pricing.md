# Gymbo pricing

Gymbo is a business app for independent personal trainers in India. Start free, and upgrade to Pro when you are ready. Pro is paid on the web with Razorpay.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Billing | Effective per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | none | ₹0 |
| Pro, monthly | ₹399 / month, incl. GST | billed monthly | ₹399 |
| Pro, annual | ₹2,999 / year, incl. GST | billed yearly | ₹250 |

All Pro prices include GST.

- **Free: ₹0.** Sign up, view and export your clients' records.
- **Pro, monthly: ₹399/month, including GST.**
- **Pro, annual: ₹2,999 per year, including GST** (₹250/month effective). Save 37%.
