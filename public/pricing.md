# Gymbo pricing

Gymbo is a business app for independent personal trainers in India. One simple subscription, **unlimited clients** on every plan, and your **first 7 days free** — no card required.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Billing | Effective per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | ₹399 / month | billed monthly | ₹399 |
| Annual | ₹2,999 / year | billed yearly | ₹250 |

- **Monthly — ₹399/month**, billed monthly. Cancel anytime.
- **Annual — ₹2,999 per year** (₹250/month effective). Save 37% — lowest price, locked in. Most popular.

## What's included on every plan

- **Unlimited clients** — flat price, no per-client tiers or limits.
- One-tap session logging (punch a class) with automatic balance math.
- Payment tracking — cash or UPI — with running balances and reminders.
- Scheduling and your full client roster in one place.
- A workout builder with templates (build and assign workouts), plus an AI assistant.
- A shareable QR profile card and branded PDF statements.

## Free trial

Your **first 7 days are free**, no credit card required. After that, choose either plan above.
